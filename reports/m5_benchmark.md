## Reading these numbers

- RMSSE here is item-level over rolling 1-7 day forecasts for one store. It is not the competition's WRMSSE (12 aggregation levels, one 28-day forecast), so it is not comparable with the Kaggle leaderboard.
- M5-specific signals (SNAP days, events) are not used; `invoices` and `customers` are missing because M5 has no transaction-level data.
- Runtime: 1597 s.
